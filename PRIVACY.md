# Verona Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 23 September 2026**

Verona is a reading and writing app for iPhone. This policy explains what Verona collects, where it
is stored, who can see it, and how to delete it. It covers the Verona iOS app and the Verona service
behind it.

Verona has no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no third-party trackers. Nothing you
read or write is sold, rented, or shared for advertising.

## Using Verona without an account

You can read, keep a library, highlight, and write without signing in. In that case everything stays
on your iPhone in the app's own storage, and Verona's servers hold nothing about you. Books you open
are downloaded from Project Gutenberg and cached on your device.

## What Verona stores when you sign in

Signing in uses **Sign in with Apple**. Apple gives Verona an account identifier, an email address,
and — the first time only — the name you choose to share. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", Verona
only ever sees the private relay address.

Once you are signed in, these are synced to Verona's servers so they follow you between devices:

- **Your reading**: books on your shelves, reading progress and page counts, the days you read,
  ratings and reviews, highlights and the notes attached to them, and any Kindle highlights you import.
- **Your writing**: manuscripts, chapters, revisions, notes, and short pieces, along with who you
  have allowed to read them.
- **Community activity**: your display name and profile details, who you follow, clubs you join and
  posts you make, messages you send, comments, likes and saves, and anyone you block or report.
- **Verification applications**, if you apply as a writer or an agent: your pen name, the writing
  sample you send (including any PDF you attach), your Amazon author page or website, any ISBNs, and
  for agents your agency, agency email, professional listings and social profiles.
- **A Sign in with Apple token**, kept only so that deleting your account can also revoke Verona's
  access to your Apple account. It is never used for anything else.

Your profile photo, bookmarks, clippings and downloaded book text stay on your device and are not
uploaded.

## Who can see what

- **Private by default.** Manuscripts and unpublished pieces are visible only to you until you choose
  to share them. Highlights, notes and reading progress are yours alone.
- **What you publish** is visible to the people you publish it to, as shown in the app.
- **Messages** are readable by you and the person you are writing to. They are not end-to-end
  encrypted: Verona's database holds them, so the administrator could in principle read them.
  Please do not send anything you would not want stored on a server.
- **Verification applications**, including writing samples and uploaded PDFs, are readable only by
  you and by Verona's administrator, for the purpose of reviewing your application.
- **The administrator** can see reports, applications, and anything needed to keep the community
  safe. No one else can read your private work.

Access rules are enforced on the server itself, row by row, so another member cannot request your
data even if they try.

## Where your data is stored

Verona uses [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy) for its database, sign-in, and file storage.
Data is stored in the United States. Files you upload (writing-sample PDFs) sit in a private bucket
that only you and the administrator can read. All traffic between the app and the server uses HTTPS.

## Other services Verona contacts

When you browse or open a book, Verona requests information from these services. They see the
request and your device's IP address, but never your Verona account or your writing:

- **Project Gutenberg** — the full text of public-domain books.
- **Open Library / Internet Archive** — book details, covers, subjects, first lines, and previews.
- **Google Books** — publisher descriptions and previews.
- **Apple** — Sign in with Apple.
- **Amazon** — Verona only links out. If you buy a Kindle book or open one in the Kindle app, that
  happens on Amazon under Amazon's own privacy policy. Verona never sees your Amazon account,
  purchases, or payment details, and it cannot read your Kindle library.

Each service has its own privacy policy. Verona does not send them your identity.

## Children

Verona is not directed to children under 13, and accounts are intended for readers 13 and older. If
you believe a child has created an account, contact us and it will be removed.

## Your choices

- **Privacy controls** in Profile: turn messages off, turn comments on your writing off, block
  anyone, and report anything that crosses the line.
- **Delete your account** in Profile → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your
  library, reading history, reviews, highlights, manuscripts, published work, follows, messages,
  comments, verification applications and uploaded PDFs from Verona's servers, clears the data on
  your device, and revokes Verona's Sign in with Apple access. It cannot be undone, so copy out any
  writing you want to keep first.
- **Take your writing with you.** There is no one-tap export yet; you can copy your work out of the
  Write tab at any time. If you want an export, ask and it will be built.

## Retention

Your data is kept until you delete it or delete your account. Deleting a piece of writing, a
highlight, or a book removes it from Verona's servers on the next sync.
